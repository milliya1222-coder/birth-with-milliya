Please note

  • There is no fixed number of weeks. Wounds, bleeding and recovery differ for everyone, so we decide together before booking where to begin.
  • Yoni steam and the full herbal bath are traditional herbal rituals, not medical treatment. Steam and hot water carry a risk of burns and irritation; they are not arranged straight away if there is a wound, bleeding, infection, pain or any other concern, and you may be asked to check with your doctor or midwife first.
  • If you have heavy bleeding, high blood pressure, or a wound that is red, swollen or inflamed, please pause, see a doctor, and we will book once things are stable.
  • This quiz is a first suggestion only; we confirm the details together beforehand. This is care and rest for body and mind, not a substitute for medical treatment.
