What I'll point you to elsewhere

These matter, and other specialists do them well, so we won't spend class time repeating them.

Best learned elsewhere

  • Your hospital's procedures and settingHospital parenting classes and a tour of the birth unit are the most accurate
  • NTUNHS "Our Family's Birth Education Class"A couples' class run by the Department of Nursing-Midwifery and Women's Health: 12 hours over 4 Saturdays, fully subsidised if you attend every session. Taught in Mandarin. Register early, as each round takes only 8 couples
  • Government prenatal educationFree, offered during your prenatal check-ups; do make use of it
  • Detailed breastfeeding skillsAn International Board Certified Lactation Consultant (IBCLC) or hospital breastfeeding class
  • Infant CPR and first aidHands-on classes from the Red Cross and others
  • Medical tests and medication in pregnancyAlways follow your doctor or midwife

What my classes focus on

  • Starting from your fears and worriesNot a packaged course; the questionnaire listens to you first
  • Physiological birth and hormonesYour body already knows how to give birth. The clearer it is, the less frightening
  • Practised in the body, not just learned in the headEmbodyBirth and BellydanceBirth practice: movement and breath that bring you back into full connection with your body, deep trust in your body, meditation and the art of surrender
  • Preparing the heartWorking through fear, meeting pain, looking back at your own birth
  • Birth vision boardFocusing on the dream birth you want to create and putting your attention on bringing it to life
  • Blessingway ceremonyPlanned around what you'd like: a gentle blessing for you and your baby
  • A prepared partnerKnowing what they can do, so they stay calm on the day
  • A birth plan for hospital or homeIn English and Chinese, so your choices are clear and heard
  • Preparing for postpartum recoveryCan continue into Ayurvedic postpartum care
