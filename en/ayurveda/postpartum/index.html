Please note

  • After a vaginal birth, local warmth can begin from one week. After a caesarean, wait until the incision has fully healed (about three to four weeks) before warmth on the abdomen or belly binding.
  • If you have heavy bleeding, high blood pressure, or a wound that is red, swollen or inflamed, please pause and we will book once things are stable.
  • This quiz is a first suggestion only; we confirm the details together beforehand. This is care and rest for body and mind, not a substitute for medical treatment.
